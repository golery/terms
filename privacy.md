Affiliate: An entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party (where "control" means ownership of 50% or more of shares or equity).

2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Data
Types of Data Collected
Personal Data
While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

First and last name

Email address

Account login details

Usage Data & Mobile Device Information
Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include Your Device's IP address, device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile browser type, and crash/diagnostic logs.

Information Collected via Device Permissions
To provide specific features within Our Application, We may request Your permission to collect:

Location Data: To deliver location-based services or content customization.

Camera & Photo Library Access: To allow picture uploads or feature interactions.

Storage Notice: This information may be uploaded to Our servers and/or a Service Provider's servers, or stored locally on Your device. You can enable or disable permission access at any time through Your Device settings.

3. Use & Sharing of Your Personal Data
We use Personal Data to provide and maintain the Service, manage Your Account, perform contracts, send security alerts or administrative updates, and fulfill legal duties.

We may share Your personal information in the following situations:

With Service Providers: To monitor analytics, process payments, or host application data.

For Business Transfers: In connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of Our business.

With Affiliates & Business Partners: Requiring them to honor this Privacy Policy.

With Other Users: When You share information publicly in community areas of the app.

With Your Consent: For any other purpose disclosed to You prior to collection.

4. International Transfer of Personal Data
Your information, including Personal Data, is processed at the Company's operating offices and on servers operated by third-party Service Providers. This means data may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of Your state, province, or country where data protection laws may differ. Your consent to this Privacy Policy represents Your agreement to such transfers with adequate security controls in place.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain Your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Account Deletion: You can request account and data deletion by navigating to [Settings > Account > Delete Account] in the app or by contacting Us at [Contact Email].

Usage Data Retention: Retained generally for internal analysis and security improvement for shorter periods, unless legally required longer.

6. Legal Disclosures & Law Enforcement
Under certain conditions, the Company may be required to disclose Your Personal Data if required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

Comply with a legal obligation or valid law enforcement request.

Protect and defend the rights or property of the Company.

Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service.

Protect the personal safety of Users of the Service or the public.

Protect against legal liability.

7. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If We learn We have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, We take immediate steps to remove that information from Our servers.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any material changes by:

Posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Sending an email and/or placing a prominent notice inside the Application prior to the change becoming effective.
